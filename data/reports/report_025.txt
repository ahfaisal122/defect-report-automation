Hi Quality Team,

I am writing to report a major issue we caught yesterday on 21 Oct 2026 during the Wiring check station for production order SG-2026-0016. We were working on the DS-72-2000 disconnector components supplied by Moravia Kontakt s.r.o. and noticed that 2 of the fasteners were not tightened to the specified torque. This is a critical assembly step, so we stopped work on those units immediately to prevent them from moving down the line.

Additionally, we found a second major problem during the Main assembly station on the same order. This time it involves part number GK-420-1184, which are the 560 mm O-ring seals provided by Halden Seals Sp. z o.o. Upon checking the components, we found that the hole pattern on 24 of these seals is offset from the drawing, making them completely unusable in their current state.

Please let us know how you would like us to handle the quarantine and return process for both batches so we can keep the shop floor moving.
