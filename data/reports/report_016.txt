Subject: Defective flange gaskets SG-2026-0498

Hi Quality Team,

Yesterday on 12.09.2026 during incoming inspection for production order SG-2026-0498, I found metal swarf embedded inside the batch of GK-245-3556 flange gaskets we received from Halden Seals Sp. z o.o.

Out of the delivery, exactly 10 pieces are affected by this contamination. Because these are critical sealing components for 245 kV equipment, these parts are completely unusable and will fail to function properly under high voltage conditions.

We definitely need immediate replacements and a corrective action from the supplier, otherwise this issue is going to delay the entire assembly schedule for this order.
